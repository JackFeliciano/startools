## Roadmap

The project currently holds a company model with six investment tiers, their amounts, percentages and investor results. Five scenarios move the model, with eleven working areas organised under the tiers. The hand-written test runner contains 37 checks.

1. Develop the relationships between investment tiers so the model presents the movement from bootstrap through to exit.
2. Expand the five scenarios to show how different inputs affect the company model and investor results.
3. Improve the presentation of amounts, percentages and results within each investment tier.
4. Connect the eleven working areas more clearly to the investment tiers they support.
5. Extend the test runner to check tier calculations, scenario outcomes and the relationships between working areas.
